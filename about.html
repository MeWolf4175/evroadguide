About EV Road Guide

Exploring the World of Electric Vehicles

Our Purpose

EV Road Guide is an educational website designed to provide information about electric vehicles and their growing role in transportation.

The goal of this website is to help visitors understand how electric cars work, their advantages and disadvantages, and the options available to people interested in electric vehicle ownership.

What You Can Learn

  • The history and development of electric cars.
  • The benefits and limitations of electric vehicles.
  • How electric vehicle charging works.
  • Where to purchase electric vehicles.
  • How to find public charging stations.
  • What to consider when installing a home charger.

About This Project

EV Road Guide was developed as an educational web development project.

The website uses HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript to create an informative, accessible, and easy-to-navigate experience.
